## About Couples counselling (for specific issues)

We Offer counselling services tailored to couples facing specific challenges, such as communication issues, infidelity, intimacy problems, or navigating major life transitions together.

## Symptoms of Couples counselling

[

Communication problems](#collapse-6c3aab06adc6b18839e6)

Difficulty expressing thoughts and feelings, frequent misunderstandings, or conflicts arising from poor communication.

[

Persistent conflict](#collapse-32030356adc6b18839e6)

Frequent arguments, unresolved issues, or feeling stuck in repetitive patterns of disagreement.

[

Emotional distance](#collapse-eb622966adc6b18839e6)

Feeling disconnected or emotionally detached from your partner, lack of intimacy, or feeling like you're drifting apart.

[

Trust issues](#collapse-ad970b96adc6b18839e6)

Infidelity, suspicion, or betrayal that has damaged trust in the relationship.

[

Sexual problems](#collapse-b9afec06adc6b18839e6)

Discrepancies in sexual desire, difficulties with intimacy, or unresolved sexual issues causing tension in the relationship.

[

Life transitions](#collapse-e8e7ad86adc6b18839e6)

Stressful life events such as moving, job changes, having children, or adjusting to changes in family dynamics.
