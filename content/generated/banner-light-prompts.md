## Final prompt set

### Healthcare

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: a bright, light-tone production website hero background for a healthcare business consultancy. Create wide 16:9 landscape editorial photography, preferably 2560x1440. NEW ART DIRECTION: light, airy, sunlit, clean PURE WHITE background; fresh white and restrained emerald green accents. No dark forest-green background, no dramatic dark lighting, no miniature buildings or toy-like 3D models. CRITICAL LAYOUT: the first 65–68% on the LEFT must be completely empty plain pure WHITE (#ffffff), absolutely no objects, writing, patterns, strong shadows, floor lines or texture. That left region is reserved for dark-green HTML copy. ALL subject detail tightly composed on the RIGHT, roughly x=72–96%, y=25–82%; top 20% stays empty white for the navbar. Photorealistic materials, premium clinical warmth, gentle daylight and only soft local contact shadows beneath right-side objects. No text, logos, watermarks, numbers, UI, charts, gradients, cream background, gray background or floating icons. Keep the left clean and bright; show the related image clearly on the RIGHT. Subject: a refined real healthcare desk still life on the right: an emerald-green stethoscope on a clean white medical worktop, a white tablet angled away with a blank unmarked screen, and a small fresh green plant in a white ceramic pot. A suggestion of a bright contemporary clinic window at the extreme right only. Real-size professional medical instruments, bright natural photography, not a hospital model or illustration.
