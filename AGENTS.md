## Approved site-wide visual direction

- Follow the background treatment approved on 2026-10-24 throughout the site: soft emerald/forest gradients with subtle, flowing curved lines and ample clear space around content.
- Use `src/components/editorial/CTA.jsx` as the reference implementation and `DESIGN-PORT.md` for design context. Adapt the treatment to light and dark sections without crowding the text.
- Do not introduce gold accents or the previous repeating diamond/circle lattice (`JaaliField`) pattern. This direction supersedes those details in the original Parvath reference.
- Preserve BoldFuse's logo and content. Use the section-heading wording from boldfuse.in.

## Site-wide typography

- Use a minimum medium (500) weight for body text, descriptions, navigation, labels, controls, statistics and footer text across all pages. Avoid light or regular weights.
- Keep headings in Manrope Bold (700), descriptions and testimonials in Nunito Sans Medium (500), and preserve the established font families for other UI text.

## Buttons and controls

- Keep all buttons and button-like links flat and static: no shadows, inherited text shadows, shimmer, lift/scale effects, moving arrows, or animated hover transitions. Preserve keyboard focus indicators.

## Section backgrounds

- Use white backgrounds for light page sections across the entire site. Do not use the pale green (`bg-cream` / `#f3f8f5`) as a section background. Keep the approved dark emerald panels and restrained green accents.
